IBM Plex Sans

Self-hosted, unmodified variable WOFF2 files from Google Fonts (v23).
Regular and true italic, weight range 400–700, normal width (100%).
Latin and Latin Extended subsets are declared separately in liquid-glass.css.
The regular Latin subset is preloaded; other subsets/styles load as needed.

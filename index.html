About

I study how to make software and AI systems more dependable.

I am a postdoctoral researcher at the TAU Lab at Università della Svizzera italiana (USI), led by Prof. Paolo Tonella.

I completed my Ph.D. at KAIST under the supervision of Prof. Shin Yoo. My research bridges software engineering and artificial intelligence, with a focus on testing, reliability, robustness, interpretability, and assurance for learning-enabled systems.

  • Software testing
  • AI assurance
  • Learning-enabled systems
  • Robustness & interpretability
